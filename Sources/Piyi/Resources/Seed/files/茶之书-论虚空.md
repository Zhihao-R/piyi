# 茶之书 · 论虚空

冈仓天心，《茶之书》（The Book of Tea，1906），第三章「道与禅」。

> This Laotse illustrates by his favourite metaphor of the Vacuum. He claimed that only in vacuum lay the truly essential. The reality of a room, for instance, was to be found in the vacant space enclosed by the roof and the walls, not in the roof and walls themselves. The usefulness of a water pitcher dwelt in the emptiness where water might be put, not in the form of the pitcher or the material of which it was made. Vacuum is all potent because all containing. In vacuum alone motion becomes possible.

老子爱用「虚」来打比方。他说，真正要紧的东西只在虚空里。比如一间屋子，它的实在是屋顶和墙围出来的那片空，而不是屋顶和墙本身；一只水罐的用处，在能盛水的那块空里，而不在罐子的样子和材料。虚因为能容纳一切，所以无所不能；只有在虚空里，才有动的可能。

> In art the importance of the same principle is illustrated by the value of suggestion. In leaving something unsaid the beholder is given a chance to complete the idea and thus a great masterpiece irresistibly rivets your attention until you seem to become actually a part of it. A vacuum is there for you to enter and fill up the full measure of your aesthetic emotion.

在艺术里，同一个道理体现在「暗示」的价值上。留下一些不说，看的人才有机会把意思补完；所以一件伟大的作品会牢牢抓住你，直到你仿佛成了它的一部分。那片空，是留给你走进去、用自己的审美感受把它填满的。
